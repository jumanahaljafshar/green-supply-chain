# Green Supply Chain

> Sustainable supply chain concept focused on waste reduction, resource efficiency, circular practices, and green logistics.

## Overview

This project explores how sustainable practices can be integrated into supply chain operations through recycled materials, improved resource utilization, and practical waste-management solutions.

## Objectives

- Reduce material waste
- Improve resource utilization
- Support circular supply chain practices
- Promote sustainable logistics solutions
- Connect sustainability with practical supply chain operations

## Project Highlights

- Researched sustainable supply chain practices
- Designed a waste-sorting concept using recycled materials
- Developed and evaluated a practical prototype
- Connected the concept to green logistics and circular economy principles

## Skills Applied

- Supply Chain Management
- Sustainable Logistics
- Process Improvement
- Research & Analysis
- Problem Solving
- Project Development
